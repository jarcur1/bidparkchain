# Pilot scope and auction decision record

**Status: proposed for product and architecture review, not approved for deployment.** This record addresses [issue #1](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/1) within the [roadmap](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/26). Review and merge approve only the policy baseline, not pilot operation or a shared deployment; the separate go/no-go decision below is required. The active prototype remains local-only. Neither this document nor a testnet deployment authorizes real bookings, physical access, or real funds. The [booking and escrow policy](booking-policy.md) specifies the proposed protected-booking matrix and deadlines; those protections require a versioned implementation, not an assertion about the current contract.

## Market and operator

The initial shared-testnet pilot is an invite-only rehearsal in Austin, Texas. Listings are synthetic descriptions of plausible private spaces, not offers of actual parking. Participants must not park, deliver access, exchange real funds, or rely on a reservation for physical access. Do not submit personal data, private access instructions, gate codes, or real access information in listings, metadata, on-chain transactions, or other pilot content. Public-chain data is public and durable; app moderation cannot erase it.

The BidPark operator enrolls invited test hosts and drivers, moderates the application and handles participant support. The operator does not take custody of participants' funds or have the ability to rewrite a deployed contract. Invitation and moderation apply to the app, not the public chain: anyone can inspect the chain and call a deployed public contract directly. The testnet deployment must not be presented as permissioned at contract level merely because the app is invite-only.

Any later real-inventory decision is limited to lawful, authorized private off-street spaces whose host has control and permission to offer them. Public curb-space resale is excluded unless separately authorized and separately decided. A listing or wallet address does not verify ownership, permission, or availability.

## Network, asset, fees, and confirmation

The single proposed shared-pilot network is **Ethereum Sepolia L1, chain ID 11155111**, using only faucet-funded native Sepolia test ETH. No token is introduced, no platform fee is charged, and no real funds or transactions intended to transfer economic value are permitted. The transaction sender pays its own test-ETH gas, including a person manually settling an auction. The operator pays gas only for its own separate deployment and operational transactions; there is no app relayer or sponsored transaction service in the early pilot. Test ETH must not be bought, sold, or treated as payment for parking.

Profiles must bind the chain ID, RPC, contract and deployment identity consistently. The UI may show pending or unfinalized activity as such, but must represent an action as confirmed only after it is reflected in finalized canonical chain state; it must handle reorgs and unavailable finality without presenting uncertain state as confirmed. Never fall back to Ethereum mainnet or automatically switch to another network. If the network or required finality is unavailable, stop the affected operations and show their status rather than guessing.

Before any shared deployment, verify that Sepolia and the required clients, RPC, faucet and finality support cover the intended operating window, including planned protocol upgrades. The [Ethereum network documentation](https://ethereum.org/en/developers/docs/networks/#sepolia) recommends Sepolia for application development; [Sepolia's network site](https://sepolia.dev/) provides network resources. The older [March 2025 Ethereum Foundation update](https://blog.ethereum.org/2025/03/18/hoodi-holesky) projected a 2026 Sepolia retirement window, while newer network guidance describes continued Sepolia use and the [2026 Glamsterdam testnet announcement](https://blog.ethereum.org/2026/09/17/glamsterdam-testnet-announcement) scheduled a Sepolia upgrade for October 2026; that schedule is not proof the upgrade occurred. These sources do not establish a confirmed retirement date or a guaranteed pilot lifetime. Resolve the support window with current authoritative guidance before deployment; if it cannot be verified, stop and seek a new explicit decision rather than falling back.

## Auction rules for rehearsal

- A host sets a positive opening bid and a fixed closing instant, `bidEnd`. The first bid must be at least the opening bid; every subsequent bid must be at least the current highest bid plus **1 wei**. The host cannot bid on their own auction.
- Each bid sends the full new amount, not just the increment. The displaced high bid is credited for a separate pull withdrawal, including when the same bidder raises their own bid. The leading bid remains escrowed and cannot be withdrawn while leading.
- A bid is eligible only when its transaction executes in a block with `block.timestamp < bidEnd`. At or after the cutoff, bidding closes; there is no overtime or automatic extension. A transaction submitted before the cutoff may still fail if included too late.
- A host may cancel an unbid listing before the cutoff under pilot policy. The prototype contract also permits cancellation after the cutoff if it remains unbid and unsettled; the policy limit is not enforced by that contract. After a bid, its current contract has no host cancellation path.
- An auction with no bids expires without a winner or funds to pay out. The prototype's permissionless `settle` can still mark an unbid auction settled and emit a zero-address winner; that does not create a winner, booking, or payout. For an auction with bids, anyone may call `settle` at or after `bidEnd`; the highest bidder is recorded as winner, subject to finalized canonical state.

The current escrow pays the host the winning amount only when settled and at or after `parkingEnd`, and has no automatic seller-failure refund or dispute process. It is suitable only for authorized illustrative rehearsals after the shared-pilot infrastructure gates below, not for real booking protection. Settlement records an on-chain result; it does not establish physical access or delivery. The future protected policy, including winning, cancellation, seller failure, no-shows, disputes, refunds, overruns and payout eligibility, is specified in the [booking policy matrix](booking-policy.md) and must be implemented and reviewed under [#18](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/18).

For future protected auctions, the bid cutoff must be at least **30 minutes before** the reservation start (`parkingStart - bidEnd >= 30 minutes`). The current contract accepts `parkingStart == bidEnd` and does not enforce this lead time; do not represent it as an existing safeguard.

## Time and reservation semantics

Store auction cutoff and reservation start/end as UTC instants. Display each instant with its local date, time, IANA property time zone and UTC offset; for Austin the property zone is `America/Chicago`. Convert from the property's zone, not the viewer's device zone. Reject nonexistent local times in daylight-saving spring-forward gaps; for ambiguous fall-back times require an explicit valid UTC offset to select the intended instant. Use a half-open reservation window `[parkingStart, parkingEnd)`: the start is included and the end is excluded. The on-chain reservation and its time window do not verify physical access or delivery. Implementation of these time semantics belongs in the relevant work on inventory and reservation handling ([#12](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/12)) and time-zone-aware presentation ([#20](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/20)); this policy does not assert that either is complete.

## Release gates

No shared deployment is approved by this record. Before an authorized illustrative shared-testnet rehearsal, review and satisfy the relevant acceptance criteria and operational readiness of:

- [#2](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/2): explicit persistent testnet profiles, no demo signing keys, verified deployment identity and confirmation policy.
- [#3](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/3): durable metadata scoped to chain, contract and auction.
- [#4](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/4): isolated regression suites and CI.
- [#8](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/8) and [#9](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/9): truthful failed/stale reads and removal of prompts to publish access information.
- [#10](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/10) and [#11](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/11): recoverable transaction operations and authenticated wallet sessions/onboarding.
- [#23](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/23) and [#24](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/24): hosted deployment safeguards and backup/restore and recovery readiness.

After all shared-pilot gates have been satisfied and evidenced, an authorized project owner must record a separate explicit shared-testnet go/no-go decision before operation. The decision must identify the target chain and deployed contract, name the operator and support owner, and reference evidence for every shared-pilot gate above. It must also record the exact disclosure presented to participants: **“This is an illustrative testnet rehearsal, not a real booking. No parking or physical access is provided. Only faucet-funded Sepolia test ETH is used; it is test currency, not payment for parking.”** A no-go, missing evidence, or missing decision means the pilot must not operate.

Before any real booking or real funds, a separate product, legal and operational approval and independent security go/no-go review are required, in addition to the shared-testnet gates and the applicable roadmap requirements. In particular, require authoritative inventory and overlap protection ([#12](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/12)), verified host control and moderation ([#13](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/13)), versioned enforceable booking protections ([#18](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/18)), private arrival information and check-in ([#19](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/19)), reliable settlement operations ([#22](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/22)), and independent security and operational review ([#25](https://github.com/jarcur1/bidparkchain/issues/25)). Links identify work to be verified, not completed safeguards or release approval. Any real-money network and asset require a separate explicit decision; this testnet choice does not authorize them.
